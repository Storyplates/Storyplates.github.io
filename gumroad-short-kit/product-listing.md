# Gumroad product listing draft

## Name
Phone Tips Shorts Studio - Daily Creator Kit

## One-line summary
Build clearer, original Android-tip Shorts with source-linked ideas, reusable scripts, caption beats, relevant tags, and a fresh daily creator brief.

## Description
Make one useful phone-tip Short at a time. Phone Tips Shorts Studio gives solo creators a focused place to choose a topic, open its official Android Help source, draft a warm voiceover, plan four short visual beats, and check the finished video before publishing.

Included:
- A curated library of source-linked Android help topics
- A new daily creator brief in each release
- A reusable prompt builder for original narration
- Four-beat caption planning
- Relevant-tag guidance
- A quick source, originality, clarity, and rights checklist
- Offline access after download

This is a creator workflow and draft library, not a promise of views, sales, or YouTube monetization. Android menu names and features can vary by device and version. Review every script against its linked official source before recording.

## Suggested price to test
$5 USD one-time, with a free 3-day sample as the storefront preview. Review actual visits and paid sales after the first week before changing the price or expanding the offer.
