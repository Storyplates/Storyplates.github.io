PeakCut’s Android AI Office is a personal workflow that researches public Android help pages, generates original explanatory videos, and can publish them to the owner’s YouTube channel.

Automated publishing

When enabled, the workflow runs on its published schedule and uses the visibility setting configured by the channel owner. The owner can stop scheduled runs in GitHub Actions and can revoke YouTube access from their Google Account.

Review and responsibility

Generated scripts and videos may contain errors. The channel owner is responsible for reviewing facts, titles, descriptions, rights, privacy settings, and compliance with YouTube rules before publication. Audience growth and monetization are not guaranteed.

Changes and contact

These terms may change if the workflow or its services change. For questions, use the support contact shown on the PeakCut Google sign-in and consent screen.
