# Free sample: 3 phone-tip Shorts starting points

Use these as briefs, not finished claims. Open the official page and check the exact steps on your own device before recording.

## 1. Check camera or microphone activity

- Viewer question: How can I tell when an app is using my camera or mic?
- First action: Look for Android's green privacy indicator while either sensor is active.
- Source: https://support.google.com/android/answer/13532937
- Prompt: Explain where the indicator appears, how a viewer can tap it to identify the app or service, and how to check Quick Settings controls. Mention Android 12+ where relevant and say device layouts may vary.

## 2. Set a screen lock

- Viewer question: What is one quick step that helps protect a phone from casual access?
- First action: Open Settings and look for Security, then Screen lock.
- Source: https://support.google.com/android/answer/9079129
- Prompt: Explain that options differ by device, describe the supported screen-lock choices from the source, and avoid claiming any lock is unbreakable.

## 3. Review Play Protect

- Viewer question: Where can someone check Google Play Protect on Android?
- First action: Open Google Play and find the Play Protect area.
- Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2812853
- Prompt: Use the source for the exact current path and explain what the scan can check. Do not guarantee that a scan catches every threat.

## 20-second script structure

1. Ask a real question.
2. Show one action on screen.
3. Explain why the action helps.
4. Invite the viewer to try it and comment without sharing private details.

## Before recording

- Open the official source and confirm every detail.
- Record your own phone screen or use original graphics.
- Use music only when you have permission or a valid license.
- Read the voiceover aloud and remove anything that sounds generic.
